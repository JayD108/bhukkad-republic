# Bhukkad Republic: concept and voice guide

## The pitch
**Bhukkad Republic is a fun, imaginary food country.** Get your passport, post what you cooked or ate, read how your
favourite dishes began, and catch the latest in the Daily Tadka.

20-second version for a visitor:
1. Everyone who arrives gets a **passport** (a fun profile card). It fills up with a **stamp for every dish** you cook, eat or try.
2. **Cook and post.** Share your plates, ask for help, swap hacks and recipes in the **Chowk**.
3. **Read if you want.** Every dish has a **file** with its history, its different styles, and the journey of its ingredients. Facts come with sources.

## What it is NOT (and where we say so)
It is a game and a food-history project. It is **not** a real country, state, government, political party or official body.
Our "passport" is a profile card, **not a travel document or ID**. The "Parliament" and "The Great Bhukkad" have no real powers.
We never claim any legal status. We respect every nation's sovereignty and food heritage.

Where this is shown (keep all of these):
- A tag in the footer of **every** page.
- An "About and fine print" page (`#/about`) linked from the footer, the passport, the Parliament and the home page.
- "Fun profile · not a travel document" printed on the passport page and on the dish-file cover.
- "Just for fun" line under the home page headline.
- Never use a real country's code, flag colours, emblem or real passport layout. (We changed our code line from `BHR`, which is Bahrain,
  to `XBK`, which is in the range ISO reserves for private use.)

This is not legal advice. Before launching publicly, ask a lawyer about the names Republic, Parliament and Passport, and about
user content, children's data and photo licences.

## Vocabulary (use these words)
| Thing | We say | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A person who signs up | **Citizen** | Gets a passport |
| Their profile | **Passport** | Name, bowl avatar, dish stamps, points, seat progress |
| A dish's page | **Dish file** | History, styles, ingredient journeys. Facts need sources |
| A citizen's post | **Plate** | "I made this". Unverified |
| How it was made | **Recipe** | Citizen recipes are unverified |
| A recipe with a source | **Sourced recipe** | Later, after the Parliament reviews it: **Approved** |
| Ways to make one dish | **Styles** | Bihari style, Jharkhand style |
| Ingredients | **Travellers / bags** | Each has a journey: where it came from |
| Ingredient native to India | **Home-grown** | |
| Ingredient that arrived from elsewhere | **Traveller** | |
| Documented / Traditional / Disputed / Unknown | **Cleared / Folk tale / Under review / Unknown** | The four stamps |
| Place | **State** | Hostel Pradesh included (fun) |
| Suggestion box | **Bills** | Corrections, each needs a source |
| Founder | **The Great Bhukkad** (Grand Head Cook) | Fun, non-political title |

Do not use: "illegal", "detained", "guilty", "deport", "alien", "invasion". Ingredients are never punished for travelling.

## Page map (each page has one job)
- **Home:** what this is, in 20 seconds, and three doors: Read, Cook, Govern.
- **Dish files:** every dish, with a search box; each file shows its state. **Dish file:** the facts. **Traveller file:** one ingredient.
- **Chowk:** citizens' plates. **My Passport:** my stamps, plates, bills and seat progress.
- **Parliament:** bills (suggestion box) and the 50 seats. **Fun:** tests, Daily Tadka, Hostel Pradesh.
- **About and fine print:** what this is, what it is not.

## Trust tiers (so the community never muddies the facts)
1. **Citizen plate / recipe:** anyone, unverified, labelled so.
2. **Sourced recipe or fact:** has a real source, shown with a footnote.
3. **Approved:** a sourced item the Parliament has reviewed (not built yet).

## The 50 seats (DRAFT, the The Great Bhukkad will tune)
A seat needs both: **enough activity** (points) **and** at least one **accepted sourced contribution**. Activity alone is not enough,
because the Parliament is about research and authenticity. Daily caps stop point farming.

## Changes (2026-11-10)
- No "explore the states" idea any more. Visitors explore **dish files**; each file just shows which state the dish is from. We cover the history of each dish: how it began.
- Head of the Republic: **The Great Bhukkad**. The footer carries a "Supreme Commander" tag.
- Chowk posts can be "I made it" **or "I ate it"** (with where you ate it).
- **Daily Tadka** = the Republic's newspaper: news and events, top plates, dish file of the day, and fact checks.
