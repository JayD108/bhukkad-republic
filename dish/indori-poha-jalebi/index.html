Indori poha-jalebi

Steamed flattened rice with peanuts, mustard and curry leaves, topped with onion, Ratlami sev and jeeravan masala, eaten with hot jalebi. Indore's morning ritual.

Madhya Pradesh · breakfast

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

after 1947 · Folk tale (traditional)

Indori poha is believed to have been created in Indore after independence in 1947. No people are named.

10th century · Cleared (documented)

The oldest known recipe for jalebi-like fritters is in a 10th-century Arabic cookbook by Ibn Sayyar al-Warraq.

c. 1450 · Cleared (documented)

In India, a Jain work by Jinasura of about 1450 mentions jalebi at a merchant's dinner.

Ingredients

Rice, Peanut (groundnut), Mustard (seeds and greens), Chilli, Wheat, Sugarcane and jaggery, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya
