Shukto

A gently bitter-sweet medley of vegetables such as bitter gourd, potato, sweet potato, raw banana and aubergine in a light milky sauce, eaten first in a Bengali meal.

West Bengal · main

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

medieval · Cleared (documented)

Shukto is mentioned in medieval Bengali Mangal-Kavya poems and in biographies of Sri Chaitanya.

ancient · Cleared (documented)

Beginning a meal with bitter foods is an old practice promoted by Ayurvedic writers.

Ingredients

Potato, Sweet potato, Eggplant (brinjal), Banana and plantain, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya
