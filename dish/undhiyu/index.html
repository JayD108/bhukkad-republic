Undhiyu

A winter casserole from Surat of beans, small aubergines, potatoes, yam, unripe banana and fenugreek dumplings, slow-cooked with a green paste of coriander, ginger, garlic and chilli.

Gujarat · main

History

name · Cleared (documented)

The name comes from the Gujarati undhu, 'upside down', because it was traditionally cooked in earthen pots (matlu) turned upside down and buried in a fire pit.

undated · Unknown

Our source gives no date for when undhiyu began.
