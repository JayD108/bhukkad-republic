Litti chokha

Wheat dough balls stuffed with spiced sattu (roasted gram flour), roasted over a fire, eaten with chokha, a smoky mash of aubergine, tomato and potato with mustard oil.

History

ancient (claimed) · Folk tale (traditional)

Litti is said to have been a staple of the armies of Magadha because it keeps well. Our source marks this claim as needing a citation.

practice · Cleared (documented)

It is tied to religious events such as the Panchkosi yatra and the Lota-Bhanta mela around Buxar.

Ingredients

Wheat, Chickpea (gram, besan), Eggplant (brinjal), Tomato, Potato, Mustard (seeds and greens), Garlic
