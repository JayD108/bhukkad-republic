Vada pav

A spiced potato fritter in gram-flour batter, tucked into a soft bread roll with fiery garlic chutney and fried green chilli. Mumbai's cheap, fast lunch.

Maharashtra · snack

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

1966 · Folk tale (traditional)

Vada pav is commonly credited to Ashok Vaidya and Sudhakar Mhatre, who are said to have created it in Mumbai in 1966.

Ingredients

Potato, Chickpea (gram, besan), Wheat, Chilli, Garlic
