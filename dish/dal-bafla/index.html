Dal bafla

Wheat dough balls boiled and then baked, softer than Rajasthani baati, dunked in ghee and eaten with spiced dal. A Malwa favourite.

Madhya Pradesh · main

Our source calls dal bafla native to Malwa in Madhya Pradesh, while noting that parts of Rajasthan also claim it.
