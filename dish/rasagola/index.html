Rasagola / rosogolla

Balls of fresh chhena cheese simmered in light sugar syrup until spongy. Both Odisha and West Bengal claim it, and each holds its own GI for its version.

Odisha, West Bengal · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

12th–19th century · Under review (disputed)

Where rasagola began is disputed between Odisha and West Bengal.

17th century · Cleared (documented)

Historians K. T. Achaya and Chitra Banerji found no reference to cheese, including chhena, in India before the 17th century, which makes a 12th-century chhena offering hard to accept.

2017 and 2019 · Cleared (documented)

West Bengal received a GI for 'Banglar Rasogolla' on 14 November 2017, and Odisha one for 'Odisha Rasagola' on 29 July 2019. The tags cover each state's version, not the question of origin.

Ingredients

Chhena and paneer (milk-splitting), Sugarcane and jaggery, Wheat
