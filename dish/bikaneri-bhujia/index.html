Bikaneri bhujia

Thin, crisp strands of moth-bean and gram-flour dough, spiced with pepper and chilli and deep-fried. Bikaner's best-known snack, made largely as a cottage industry.

Rajasthan · snack

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

1877 · Folk tale (traditional)

The first batch is commonly dated to 1877, in the princely state of Bikaner during the reign of Maharaja Dungar Singh.

2010 · Cleared (documented)

Bikaneri Bhujia is a registered Geographical Indication of Rajasthan. A news report from September 2010 lists it among twelve newly registered products.

Ingredients

Moth bean, Chickpea (gram, besan), Chilli, Black pepper, Green cardamom
