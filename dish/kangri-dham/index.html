Kangri dham

A festive meal of the Kangra valley served in courses: rice, madra (chickpeas in yogurt), black lentils, a sweet-sour pumpkin khatta and sweet rice. No onion or garlic.

Himachal Pradesh · festival

History

practice · Cleared (documented)

Dham is cooked by hereditary cooks called botis for temple festivals, weddings and religious ceremonies, and is strictly satvik, with no onion or garlic.

undated · Unknown

Our source gives no date for when the tradition began.

Ingredients

Rice, Chickpea (gram, besan), Black gram (urad), Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Pumpkin and squash, Tamarind, Green cardamom
