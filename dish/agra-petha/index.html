Agra petha

Translucent candied pieces of ash gourd, soaked in lime water to stay firm, then boiled in sugar syrup. Agra's counters sell it in many flavours.

Uttar Pradesh · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

17th century (legend) · Folk tale (traditional)

A legend says petha was invented in Shah Jahan's kitchens to feed the Taj Mahal's workers. Our source calls this highly unlikely: petha is not in Shah Jahan's cookbook, the Nuskha-e-Shahjahani.

undated · Unknown

Its real date of invention is unknown. Similar sweets are recorded before the Mughals.

Ingredients

Ash gourd (petha), Sugarcane and jaggery
