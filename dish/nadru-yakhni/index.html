Nadru yakhni

Slices of lotus stem simmered in a creamy, gently spiced yogurt gravy. A slow, elaborate curry from the Kashmir Valley.

Jammu and Kashmir · main

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

15th century · Folk tale (traditional)

A story says lotus stem entered Kashmiri food in the 15th century, when boatmen of the ruler Zain-ul-Abidin picked lotus roots on the Gill Sar lake for his supper.

Ingredients

Lotus stem (nadru), Milk, curd, ghee and khoya
