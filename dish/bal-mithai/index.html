Bal mithai

Dark, fudge-like cubes of roasted khoya coated with tiny white sugar balls. Almora's famous sweet, nicknamed 'chocolate' for its colour.

Uttarakhand · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

early 20th century · Folk tale (traditional)

Joga Lal Sah, a sweet-maker in business in Almora's Lal Bazaar since 1865, is credited with creating bal mithai as it is known today; his descendants still run the shop.

1940s · Folk tale (traditional)

A local story says soldiers from Kumaon carried bal mithai while serving in Burma in the Second World War.

Ingredients

Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Sugarcane and jaggery, Poppy seed (khaskhas)
