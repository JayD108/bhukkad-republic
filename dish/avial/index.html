Avial

A thick mix of many vegetables, such as yam, plantain, ash gourd, drumstick and beans, cooked with ground coconut and curd and finished with coconut oil and curry leaves. A fixture of the sadhya feast.

Kerala · main

History

legend · Folk tale (traditional)

A legend credits Bhima of the Mahabharata: working as a cook in King Virata's kitchen, he chopped every vegetable he had, boiled them together and topped them with coconut.

legend · Folk tale (traditional)

Another story says a Travancore king, short of curry at a feast, had his cook use the leftover vegetables; the dish was then served at the sadhya.

Ingredients

Coconut, Banana and plantain, Ash gourd (petha), Eggplant (brinjal), Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Tamarind
