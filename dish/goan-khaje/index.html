Goan khaje

Crunchy sticks of fried gram-flour dough coated in jaggery, piled into mountains at temple zatras and church feasts across Goa.

Goa · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

practice · Cleared (documented)

Khaje is sold at the fairs held after temple zatras and church feasts.

2019–2021 · Cleared (documented)

The All Goa Khaje Producers Association applied for a GI in July 2019. A Goan paper reported the registration in July 2020; the national GI list shows the year as 2021–22.

Ingredients

Chickpea (gram, besan), Sugarcane and jaggery
