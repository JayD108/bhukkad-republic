Sarson da saag and makki di roti

Slow-cooked mustard greens with garlic, ginger and chilli, finished with ghee or butter, eaten with flatbread made from maize flour. Punjab's winter meal.

Punjab · main

History

name · Cleared (documented)

Sarson comes from the Sanskrit sarṣapa, 'mustard', and saag from śāka, 'greens'.

undated · Cleared (documented)

One source cited in our reference says mustard has been grown in Punjab for millennia; the maize in makki di roti, by contrast, is an American crop.

Ingredients

Mustard (seeds and greens), Maize (corn), Garlic, Ginger, Chilli, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya
