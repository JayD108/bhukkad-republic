Siddu

A steamed bun of yeasted wheat dough stuffed with a savoury paste of lentils, poppy seeds and walnuts, or a sweet filling, and eaten hot with ghee.

Himachal Pradesh · bread

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

Siddu is said to come from the Mahasu area (now in the former Shimla district) and to have spread to Kullu, Mandi and Sirmaur. It was traditionally made by shepherds.

Ingredients

Wheat, Black gram (urad), Poppy seed (khaskhas), Chilli, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya
