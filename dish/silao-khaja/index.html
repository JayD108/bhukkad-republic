Silao khaja

A flaky, many-layered pastry of wheat flour and khoya, deep-fried and soaked in syrup, from Silao near Nalanda. Often compared to baklava.

Bihar · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

folk tale · Folk tale (traditional)

A folk tale says the Buddha was offered khaja at Silao while travelling from Rajgir to Nalanda.

1872–73 · Cleared (documented)

Archaeologist J. D. Beglar described the Silao sweet during his survey there in 1872–73 and linked it to King Vikramaditya.

Dec 2018 · Cleared (documented)

The GI Registry granted Silao Khaja a Geographical Indication in December 2018.

Ingredients

Wheat, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Sugarcane and jaggery
