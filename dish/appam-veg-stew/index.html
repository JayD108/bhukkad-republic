Appam with vegetable stew

Bowl-shaped pancakes of fermented rice batter and coconut milk, soft in the middle and lacy at the edges, served with a mild coconut-milk vegetable stew.

Kerala · breakfast

History

Sangam period · Cleared (documented)

Food historian K. T. Achaya, as cited by journalist Vir Sanghvi, says Tamil Sangam poems describe appams sold with modakam in the street markets of Madurai.

medieval · Cleared (documented)

A 2018 study of Chola-era temple inscriptions reports an early appam recipe carved on the walls of the Srirangam temple as an offering.

name · Folk tale (traditional)

The name may come from the Sanskrit apupa, a kind of fried sweet.

Ingredients

Rice, Coconut, Potato
