Misal pav

A fiery curry of sprouted moth beans (usal) with a thin spicy gravy, topped with farsan, onion and lemon, eaten with soft bread rolls. Every Maharashtra city argues its version is best.

Maharashtra · breakfast

History

undated · Unknown

Our sources give no date or founding story for misal. They name Mamledar, Bedekar and Katakir among shops that have served it for decades.

2015 · Cleared (documented)

In 2015 the misal at Aaswad in Dadar, Mumbai, was named the world's tastiest vegetarian dish at the FoodieHub Awards in London.
