Chhena poda

Fresh chhena kneaded with sugar and semolina, then baked for hours until the outside caramelises. The name means 'burnt cheese' in Odia.

Odisha · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

after 1947 · Folk tale (traditional)

It is credited to Sudarsan Sahu, who experimented with leftover cheese at his hotel in Dashapalla, Odisha, after 1947.

2022 · Cleared (documented)

Chhenapoda Dibasa, a day celebrating the sweet, has been observed since 11 April 2022, his birth anniversary.

Ingredients

Chhena and paneer (milk-splitting), Sugarcane and jaggery, Wheat, Cashew, Green cardamom
