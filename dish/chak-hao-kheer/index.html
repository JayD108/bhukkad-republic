Chak-hao kheer

A purple-black rice pudding made by slow-cooking Manipur's aromatic black rice, chak-hao, in milk with sugar.

History

20 Apr 2020 · Cleared (documented)

Chak-hao black rice (not the kheer itself) received a Geographical Indication on 20 April 2020, after a producers' group from Imphal applied in 2017.
