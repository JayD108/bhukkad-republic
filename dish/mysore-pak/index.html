Mysore pak

A rich, porous sweet of gram flour cooked with sugar syrup and a generous amount of ghee, set and cut into blocks. Often scented with cardamom.

Karnataka · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

early 20th century · Folk tale (traditional)

Tradition says a palace cook named Kakasura Madappa created it in Mysore Palace during the reign of Krishna Raja Wadiyar IV and named it after the city.

Ingredients

Chickpea (gram, besan), Sugarcane and jaggery, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Green cardamom
