Puran poli

A soft flatbread stuffed with cooked chana dal sweetened with jaggery and scented with cardamom or nutmeg, eaten warm with ghee at festivals across Maharashtra, Karnataka and Andhra.

Maharashtra, Karnataka · sweet

History

12th century · Cleared (documented)

The Manasollasa, a 12th-century Sanskrit encyclopedia written for King Someshvara III in Karnataka, refers to the dish.

undated · Cleared (documented)

Ayurvedic works such as the Bhavaprakasha also give a recipe for it.

Ingredients

Wheat, Chickpea (gram, besan), Sugarcane and jaggery, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Green cardamom
