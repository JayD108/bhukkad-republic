Amritsari kulcha

A crisp, layered tandoor bread stuffed with spiced mashed potato, brushed with butter and usually eaten with chole. Amritsar's breakfast.

Punjab · bread

History

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

It is said to have originated in Amritsar, inspired by the Peshawari khameeri roti, a sourdough flatbread. No date is given.

Persian word · Cleared (documented)

The word kulcha comes from Persian, for a disc-shaped leavened loaf.

Ingredients

Wheat, Potato, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Chickpea (gram, besan), Chilli
