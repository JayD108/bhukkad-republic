Dharwad peda

A brown, crumbly milk sweet made by cooking milk with sugar for a long time until it caramelises, traditionally from the milk of Dharwadi buffaloes.

Karnataka · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

late 18th century onwards · Folk tale (traditional)

Family history says the Thakurs moved from Unnao in Uttar Pradesh after a plague in the late 18th century. Ram Ratan Singh Thakur began selling pedas, and his grandson Babu Singh Thakur made the Line Bazaar shop famous.

2008–09 · Cleared (documented)

Dharwad Pedha is a registered Geographical Indication of Karnataka.

Ingredients

Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Sugarcane and jaggery
