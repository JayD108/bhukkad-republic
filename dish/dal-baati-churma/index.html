Dal baati churma

Hard wheat-dough balls baked over coals and soaked in ghee, eaten with spiced lentils and churma, a sweet of crushed wheat, ghee and jaggery or sugar.

Rajasthan · main

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

8th century (claimed) · Folk tale (traditional)

Accounts link baati to the time of Bappa Rawal, founder of the Mewar kingdom, and describe it as a wartime meal of his clan.

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

A popular story says soldiers buried dough in the desert sand and let the sun bake it. Our source calls this anecdotal.

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

Two folk stories explain churma: a cook spilling cane juice on baati, or home cooks soaking baati in sweet syrup. There is no firm evidence for either.

Ingredients

Wheat, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Sugarcane and jaggery, Pigeon pea (toor, arhar), Garlic, Chilli
