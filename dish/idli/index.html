Idli

Soft, steamed cakes of fermented rice and black-gram batter, eaten with sambar and coconut chutney across South India.

Tamil Nadu, Karnataka · breakfast

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

920 CE · Cleared (documented)

The Kannada work Vaddaradhane (920 CE) mentions iddalige, made from black gram alone, with no rice.

1130 CE · Cleared (documented)

The Sanskrit Manasollasa (1130 CE) describes iddarika. These early recipes lack rice, long fermentation and steaming; recipes like today's idli appear only after about 1250 CE.

800–1200 CE? · Under review (disputed)

Where the steamed, fermented idli came from is disputed.

Ingredients

Rice, Black gram (urad)
