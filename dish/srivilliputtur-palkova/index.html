Srivilliputtur palkova

A creamy, fudge-like sweet of rich milk slowly reduced with sugar, made in the temple town of Srivilliputhur from local high-fat milk.

Tamil Nadu · sweet

Open the full dish filePost your plate

History

c. 1921 · Folk tale (traditional)

A press report traces the town's palkova to about 1921, when Dev Singh opened Lala Sweets near the Andal temple, inspired by the temple's milk prasadam.

2019–20 · Cleared (documented)

Srivilliputtur Palkova is a registered Geographical Indication of Tamil Nadu; the local milk producers' cooperative led the application.

Ingredients

Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Sugarcane and jaggery
