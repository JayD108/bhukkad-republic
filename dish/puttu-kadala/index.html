Puttu and kadala curry

Cylinders of steamed coarse rice flour layered with grated coconut, eaten with a spicy curry of black chickpeas. Kerala's classic breakfast.

History

legend · Folk tale (traditional)

In Tamil Nadu, a myth about a flood of the Vaigai river and the Pandya king Arimarthana involves puttu; it is re-enacted every year at the Avani Mula festival at the Meenakshi temple in Madurai.
