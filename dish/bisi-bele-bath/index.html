Bisi bele bath

Rice and toor dal cooked together with vegetables, tamarind and a special spice blend, finished with ghee. The name means 'hot lentil rice'.

Karnataka · rice

History

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

It is said to have begun in the Mysore Palace kitchens, where its recipe stayed close to secret among palace cooks for a long time. No dates are given.

Ingredients

Rice, Pigeon pea (toor, arhar), Tamarind, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Chilli
