Ghewar

A lacy, honeycombed disc of fried flour-and-ghee batter soaked in sugar syrup, often topped with rabri and nuts. Rajasthan's sweet for Teej and the monsoon month of Shravan.

Rajasthan · sweet

History

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

Ghewar is said to have been made first by cooks in Rajasthan's royal kitchens before it reached ordinary homes. No dates are given for this.

16th century · Cleared (documented)

Mirabai, the 16th-century poet-saint, describes offering ghevar to Krishna in one of her poems.

undated · Folk tale (traditional)

The name may come from the Sanskrit ghṛtapūra, roughly 'ghee cake'.

1961 · Cleared (documented)

Laxmi Mishthan Bhandar in Jaipur introduced a chhena (fresh cheese) version in 1961.

Ingredients

Wheat, Milk, curd, ghee and khoya, Sugarcane and jaggery, Chhena and paneer (milk-splitting), Green cardamom
